# Illustrative corpus: Structuring & Suspicious Transaction Reporting

## AML-STR-207 — Structuring indicator
A pattern of multiple cash transactions by, or on behalf of, the same customer, each
individually below a reporting threshold but aggregating to a value at or above that
threshold within a short window, is an illustrative indicator of structuring ("smurfing")
and should be escalated for review regardless of the individual transaction values.

## AML-STR-212 — Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) content
An illustrative STR should document: the customer and account(s) involved, the transactions
that triggered the review with dates, amounts, and channels, the specific indicator(s) relied
upon, the evidence considered and why alternative innocent explanations were assessed and
ruled out or could not be ruled out, and the reporting entity's final determination.

## AML-STR-219 — Timelines for escalation
Illustrative internal timeline: an alert should be triaged by a first-line analyst within 2
business days of generation, and where escalation is warranted, a determination on STR filing
should be reached within 7 business days of escalation, subject to the entity's internal
policy and applicable regulatory timelines.
