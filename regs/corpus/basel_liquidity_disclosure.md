# Illustrative corpus: Liquidity Coverage Ratio Disclosure

## BASEL-LCR-303 — Minimum ratio
Illustrative requirement: a regulated bank should maintain a Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)
of High-Quality Liquid Assets to Total Net Cash Outflows over a 30-day stress horizon of no
less than 100%, calculated and disclosed on the frequency set by the local regulator.

## BASEL-LCR-309 — Composition of High-Quality Liquid Assets
Illustrative HQLA composition guidance distinguishes Level 1 assets (e.g. cash and central
bank reserves, sovereign securities) held without haircut, from Level 2A and 2B assets which
are subject to haircuts and a cap on their share of total HQLA.

## BASEL-LCR-315 — Disclosure granularity
Illustrative disclosure expectation: quarterly average LCR should be disclosed alongside the
components of HQLA and net cash outflows in sufficient granularity for a market participant
or supervisor to assess the bank's liquidity risk profile without needing non-public data.
