# Illustrative corpus: Customer Due Diligence

## KYC-CDD-101 — Risk categorisation of customers
Regulated entities should classify each customer into a risk category (Low, Medium, High) at
onboarding, based on factors that may illustratively include: nature of business or
occupation, expected transaction volume and pattern, geographic exposure, and whether the
customer is a politically exposed person (PEP). Risk categorisation should be reviewed
periodically and whenever a material change in customer behaviour is observed.

## KYC-CDD-104 — Enhanced due diligence triggers
Enhanced due diligence is illustratively expected where a customer is rated High risk, is a
PEP or close associate of a PEP, operates through complex or opaque ownership structures, or
where transaction activity is materially inconsistent with the customer's stated profile at
onboarding.

## KYC-CDD-118 — Periodic review cadence
Illustrative periodic review intervals: High-risk customers reviewed at least annually,
Medium-risk customers at least every two years, Low-risk customers at least every three
years, unless an event-driven review is triggered earlier by unusual transaction activity.
